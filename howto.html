How to Use Snapbooth

Simple steps to capture perfect moments instantly.

1

Open Camera

Click “Open Camera” from the homepage to access your photobooth.

2

Allow Camera Access

Enable your webcam so Snapbooth can start the live preview.

3

Choose Filters

Use the left and right arrows to switch between filter effects.

4

Capture Photo

Press the capture button and wait for the countdown timer.

5

Save Image

Your photo will automatically download and save instantly.
